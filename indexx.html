History of Classical Music

Indian classical music has roots in the ancient Samaveda, where sacred verses were sung through carefully defined notes and rhythms.

Over centuries, this tradition developed into Hindustani music in North India and Carnatic music in South India.

The guru-shishya tradition passed knowledge from teacher to student through practice, discipline and devotion.
